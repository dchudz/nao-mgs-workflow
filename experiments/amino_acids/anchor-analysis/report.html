Method and reproducibility

Both NR mates must align and have combined viral-only near-best evidence. NR and viral-reference LCAs must resolve to the same species. The original pinned nao_dedup commit 404c7080e4f5a73d0fbdd02e535858c74b0ebeb0 clusters a joint pool of native retained pairs and all viral-only protein candidates, before mate/species restrictions, using real recovered qualities and deterministic sequence-hash order. Each depth and policy forms its own pool. The local implementation reproduced the previously saved worker fixture exactly.

This is the same supplemental similarity-only criterion as the 200-sample analysis. It does not reproduce the production alignment-coordinate duplicate stage, establish biological truth, or constitute an end-to-end pipeline comparison. Each read keeps its own validation verdict; no verdict is transferred through a cluster.
